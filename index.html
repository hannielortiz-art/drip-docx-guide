Nurse Guide

IV drips and injections: draw-up, bag, add-ons, cautions.

Before every IV
  • Check vitals before and after.
  • Ask if they have eaten. If not, give juice first.
  • Ask if they are nervous about needles (vasovagal risk).
  • More than 2 ingredients in one bag: use a multi-vial adapter.
Screening before every treatment
Standing rules, and who should not get what

Standing rules

  • Patients 18 and over.
  • No more than 3 IVs per week for any patient.
  • Advise plenty of water and something to eat beforehand.
  • Take vitals before and after every infusion, and chart them.

Ask before you draw anything up

  • Pregnant or breastfeeding: confirm which treatments are excluded.
  • Kidney disease: no Toradol, in any drip.
  • NSAID allergy, ulcer or GI bleed: no Toradol.
  • On warfarin or Coumadin: no Ultimate Drip (vitamin K interferes with INR).
  • Arrhythmia or heart problems: no Pepcid as a routine add-on.
  • G6PD deficiency: raise with Narges before high-dose vitamin C or glutathione.

Stop

If anything is unclear, or the patient’s history does not fit the treatment they booked, stop and call Narges before you start.
